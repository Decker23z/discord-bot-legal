# Game Master Bot — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026
**Application:** Game Master Bot
**Operator:** Christopher Decker
**Contact:** decker.cjd\@outlook.com

### 1. Overview

Game Master Bot ("the Bot") is a Discord application designed to facilitate tabletop role-playing games through artificial intelligence, automated narration, character management, and interactive voice capabilities.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Bot accesses, processes, stores, and deletes information during gameplay.

### 2. Information We Process

The Bot may process the following information when necessary to provide its features:

**Discord Information**

- Discord user IDs and display names
- Discord server and channel identifiers
- Messages and commands directed to the Bot
- Information necessary to identify participating players and campaigns

**Game Information**

- Character names, statistics, abilities, inventories, and progression
- Campaign settings, story developments, and completed objectives
- Session summaries, gameplay decisions, and persistent game-state information
- Player preferences related to gameplay

**Voice Information**

- Player voice audio during enabled voice interactions
- Temporary audio data necessary for speech recognition and AI interaction
- Transcribed player instructions when needed for gameplay

### 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed exclusively for operating and maintaining the Bot's intended functionality, including:

- Providing AI-generated Game Master narration
- Interpreting player actions and commands
- Maintaining continuity between game sessions
- Generating NPC dialogue and character voices
- Managing game rules, encounters, and character progression
- Troubleshooting technical issues when necessary

The Bot does not use Discord user information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or sale to third parties.

### 4. Campaign Data Retention

The Bot stores campaign-related information to allow players to resume games across multiple sessions.

Saved information may include character records, campaign progress, gameplay decisions, and session summaries.

Campaign information is retained while necessary to support an active campaign, subject to deletion requests and applicable legal or platform requirements.

When a campaign is deleted, its associated stored information will be removed from the Bot's active storage, except where retention is legally required. Backup deletion, if applicable, will follow the operator's documented backup retention schedule.

### 5. Voice Processing and Recordings

The Bot is designed to process player voice input during active gameplay without intentionally maintaining permanent recordings.

- Voice processing occurs only when the Bot's voice functionality is enabled.
- Audio may be transmitted to speech-processing services when necessary.
- The Bot does not intentionally archive raw player voice recordings.
- Temporary audio buffers are discarded after processing when no longer necessary.

Speech-to-text output may be used during gameplay. Information derived from voice interactions may be included in saved campaign summaries or game-state records.

Third-party voice-processing providers may independently process or temporarily retain transmitted information according to their applicable policies.

Players should be informed when the Bot is actively processing voice communications.

### 6. Third-Party Services

The Bot may use the following providers:

**Discord:** Provides the communication platform, user interactions, and server infrastructure.

**OpenAI:** May process player commands, game context, and gameplay information to generate narrative responses, decisions, and other AI-assisted content.

**ElevenLabs:** May process NPC dialogue or other content required to synthesize character voices.

Only information necessary for the relevant functionality is intended to be transmitted to these providers.

Each provider maintains its own privacy and data-handling practices. Users may review their policies:

- Discord: [https://discord.com/privacy](https://discord.com/privacy)
- OpenAI: [https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- ElevenLabs: [https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy](https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy)

### 7. Information Sharing

The Bot does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to deliver its functionality, fulfill legal obligations, or as otherwise permitted by applicable law and Discord's developer requirements.

Campaign information may be visible to other participants in the same game, depending on the campaign's configuration.

### 8. Data Security

The operator will use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed, including restricted access to stored campaign records and protection of application credentials.

No electronic service can guarantee absolute security.
